The Indian fabric guide

Choose by feel.
Build by purpose.

A quick field guide to the cloth you will commonly find across India's fabric market. Construction, weight, and finish matter as much as the fibre.

Cotton knits

Jersey · Pique · Interlock · Rib

Breathable, comfortable structures for tees, polos, everyday separates, collars, and cuffs.

Sweatshirt knits

French terry · Fleece

Loopback for a lighter layer; brushed fleece when warmth and a soft inner hand matter.

Woven cotton

Poplin · Cambric · Twill

Crisper and more structured than knits, making them useful for shirts, kurtas, uniforms, and trousers.

Fluid blends

Viscose · Rayon · Modal

Soft, fluid fabrics with an easy fall for dresses, shirts, linings, and relaxed silhouettes.

Natural texture

Linen · Cotton-linen

Airy, textured choices for warm-weather clothing, with a natural ease that improves with wear.

Structured utility

Denim · Drill · Twill

Durable woven fabrics for jeans, jackets, workwear, bags, and pieces that need more body.
